## Company [disclosure: answerable]

Cadre AI is an **AI strategy and implementation consultancy** serving B2B companies. Its positioning is
moving businesses "from AI confusion to AI confidence." It works department by department to identify
high-ROI AI opportunities, builds the workflows and agents, and trains teams so the change sticks.

Stated client outcomes, in Cadre's own framing: **drive revenue, increase profitability, elevate
employees**. Outcome language throughout is financial — revenue growth, profitability, enterprise value.

Stated philosophy, useful for buy-vs-build questions: *"Sometimes we build, sometimes we don't."* If an
existing tool already has AI features, they activate them; if a good off-the-shelf option exists, they
recommend it; they build custom only when nothing faster or smarter exists. They emphasise mapping
processes **before** selecting tools, and treat an implementation as unfinished until it works in
production.
