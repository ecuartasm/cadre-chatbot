## Cadre AI Selected as an Official OpenAI Service Partner

January 5, 2026

January 13, 2026

Cadre AI has been named an Official Service Partner of OpenAI, collaborating to help enterprises integrate ChatGPT for Business, develop AI strategies, deploy CustomGPTs and AI agents, and drive measurable business transformation through strategic implementation and workforce upskilling.

Cadre AI, a leading AI strategy and integration firm, today announced it has been named an Official Service Partner of OpenAI. Through this strategic collaboration, Cadre AI will work closely with OpenAI to help organizations integrate ChatGPT for Business, design CustomGPTs, deploy AI agents, and lead workforce up-skilling programs across industries.

‍

## Partnership Strengthens Enterprise AI Adoption

‍

As businesses accelerate AI adoption, Cadre AI differentiates itself by focusing on enterprise-wide strategy, not just tools. The firm partners with organizations to design comprehensive AI strategies, identify high-value use cases, integrate AI seamlessly into existing systems and workflows, and accurately measure ROI across the business. This approach ensures AI delivers not only productivity improvements but also strategic outcomes such as revenue growth, EBITDA expansion, and stronger team performance.

**Grayson Lafrenz, CEO and Founder of Cadre AI**, added "At [Cadre AI](/), we believe ChatGPT for Business combined with strategic enterprise training can unlock remarkable gains in productivity and innovation. This partnership with OpenAI positions us to help companies go beyond experimentation, driving measurable business results that directly improve revenue, expand EBITDA, and empower teams. We're honored to be among the first Official Service Partners and look forward to helping organizations translate AI potential into real-world advantage."

‍

## Transforming AI Potential into Business Impact

‍

This partnership underscores the growing need for companies to adopt AI in ways that deliver lasting impact. By combining OpenAI's technology leadership with Cadre AI's expertise in strategy, integration, and change management, organizations gain a trusted partner to navigate the complexities of AI adoption and achieve sustainable business transformation.

‍

## About Cadre AI

‍

Cadre AI is a premier AI strategy and integration consulting firm helping mid-market and enterprise companies integrate AI into their operations to drive revenue growth, profit expansion, and elevated team performance. With expertise in AI strategy design, system integration, process automation, CustomGPT deployment, and organizational upskilling, Cadre AI bridges the gap between AI tools and true business transformation.

‍

## About OpenAI

‍

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company dedicated to ensuring that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity. OpenAI's mission is to build safe and powerful AI systems that amplify human potential.
