## Why do these challenges exist?

Most real estate professionals struggle with manual property analysis and market research that slows deal velocity and limits how many opportunities they can pursue. Lead qualification remains reactive and time-consuming instead of instant and automated based on buyer criteria and market signals. Transaction coordination involves endless email threads and manual follow-ups rather than automated workflows that keep deals moving. Market intelligence comes from scattered sources and delayed data instead of real-time alerts on pricing trends, inventory changes, and competitive activity.
