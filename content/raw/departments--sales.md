## Why do these challenges exist?

Most sales teams struggle with manual prospecting and lead research that limits pipeline volume and steals time from actual selling. Lead qualification remains reactive and time-intensive instead of instant scoring that prioritizes the best opportunities. Quote generation and proposal creation consume days of back-and-forth that slow deal velocity and create buyer frustration. Pipeline management relies on manual CRM updates and status chasing rather than automated workflows that keep deals advancing without constant intervention.
