## Why do these challenges exist?

Most private equity firms struggle with deal sourcing that only surfaces opportunities after they hit the market, missing pre-emptive positioning on the best targets. Due diligence processes remain painfully manual - CIM analysis, financial modeling, and contract review consume weeks of partner time and slow deal velocity. Relationship intelligence lives scattered across individual email inboxes and memories instead of centralized systems that surface warm introductions when they matter most. Portfolio monitoring relies on backward-looking board presentations rather than real-time data that flags operational issues before they compound into value erosion.
