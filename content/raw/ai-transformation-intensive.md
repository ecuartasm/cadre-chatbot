Our Strategic Approach

### The AI Transformation Intensive.

Your leadership team and ours. Co-authored strategy: where you stand, where you need to go, and exactly how to get there from Kickoff to 12-Month Roadmap.

### 1. Kickoff

Cadre's leadership team and yours will meet to set the foundation for what the next 45 days holds. We'll walk through all upcoming deliverables and timelines including your AI Maturity Index, the full-day workshop, use case library, 3-year vision, and your 12-month roadmap for how to get there.

### 2. AI Maturity Index

This helps us map where your organization stands today on its AI journey. We discover the areas of excitement and fear across your team, their prefered learning modalities, how AI is currently being used, and more.

### 3. Full-Day Workshop

In this one-day workshop, we'll use initial findings from your AI Maturity Index and kickoff session to dig deep into ways AI can drive revenue, improve profitability and elevate employees. Throughout the day, we'll spend time with executive sponsors, go-to-market, revops, delivery, operations, customer support, finance, and more.

### 4. Use Case Library

After spending a full day with various members of your team along with subsequent follow-up calls, we'll curate a list of AI use cases based on our findings. In this session, your team and ours will co-author the AI solutions that feel like immediate fits, projects for the future, and those that don't drive enough value to prioritize.

### 5. Three-Year Vision

Everything we've done so far in this engagement leads us to crafting a robust three-year vision outlining how your organization could evolve given the speed AI is moving. We'll show you which roles are still human-led, which are fully automated, and how the ongoing AI shift will impact day-to-day life for your team.

### 6. Twelve-Month Roadmap

The AI Transformation Intensive wraps up with a roadmap detailing which AI initiatives and additional workflow deep dives should be prioritized over the next year to get you closer to your three-year vision. This roadmap is built specifically for the pace your organization can absorb and is designed to systematically increase your organization's AI Maturity Index score.
