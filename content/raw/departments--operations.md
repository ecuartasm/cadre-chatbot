## Why do these challenges exist?

Most operations teams struggle with manual process coordination that creates bottlenecks and slows execution across the business. Documentation remains scattered and outdated instead of centralized systems that make processes accessible and repeatable. Request routing and task assignment rely on email threads and manual handoffs rather than automated workflows that ensure nothing falls through cracks. Status tracking requires constant manual updates and chasing instead of real-time visibility that surfaces issues before they compound.
