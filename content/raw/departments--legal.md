## Why do these challenges exist?

Most legal teams struggle with contract review backlogs that delay deals and frustrate business stakeholders waiting for approvals. Legal research remains time-intensive and manual instead of instant retrieval of relevant precedents and clause language. Compliance tracking relies on spreadsheets and manual monitoring rather than automated alerts that surface regulatory changes and upcoming deadlines. Legal knowledge stays trapped in partner expertise instead of accessible systems that let the business self-serve on routine questions.
