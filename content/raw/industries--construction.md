## Why do these challenges exist?

Most construction firms struggle with manual takeoffs and estimating that consumes days of preconstruction time and introduces pricing errors that erode job margins. Project delays happen because issues aren't visible until they've already impacted the schedule, creating costly cascading effects across subcontractor coordination and material delivery. Change order management remains reactive and manual, leading to scope creep and margin erosion on jobs that should be profitable. Labor and equipment scheduling relies on static plans that can't adapt to real-time weather, material delays, or workforce availability changes.
