## Why do these challenges exist?

Most professional services firms struggle with billable hour constraints that cap revenue growth and limit scalability. Manual proposal creation, contract review, and research consume expensive partner time that should go to client strategy and business development. Knowledge management remains fragmented across individual expertise and scattered documents instead of accessible institutional systems. Client communication and project status tracking still rely on time-intensive manual updates rather than automated workflows that keep everyone aligned.
