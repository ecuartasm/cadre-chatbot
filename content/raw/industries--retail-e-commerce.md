## Why do these challenges exist?

Most retail and e-commerce operations struggle with generic product recommendations that miss personalization opportunities and leave revenue on the table. Inventory forecasting remains reactive and inaccurate, leading to stockouts that lose sales or overstock that erodes margin through markdowns. Customer service inquiries overwhelm support teams with repetitive questions that delay response times and hurt satisfaction scores. Returns and fraud detection happen after losses have occurred rather than through predictive signals that prevent problems before checkout.
