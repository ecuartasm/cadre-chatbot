## Why do these challenges exist?

Most finance teams struggle with manual reconciliation and month-end close processes that consume days of tedious work and delay financial reporting. Financial forecasting remains backward-looking and static instead of dynamic models that update with real-time business performance. Expense management and approval workflows rely on manual review and routing rather than automated validation that accelerates reimbursement. Budget variance analysis happens after the fact through spreadsheet archaeology instead of real-time alerts that flag issues when intervention is still possible.
