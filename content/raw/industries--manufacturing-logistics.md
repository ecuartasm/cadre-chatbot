## Why do these challenges exist?

Most manufacturing and logistics operations struggle with reactive maintenance that results in unexpected downtime and expensive emergency repairs. Inventory management remains manual and reactive, leading to stockouts that halt production or excess inventory that ties up working capital. Production scheduling relies on static plans that don't adapt to real-time changes in demand, materials, or equipment availability. Supply chain visibility is fragmented across systems and partners, making it impossible to predict delays or optimize routing until problems have already impacted delivery timelines.
