## Why do these challenges exist?

Most marketing teams struggle with content creation bottlenecks that limit campaign velocity and force choices between quality and volume. Campaign performance analysis remains manual and retrospective instead of real-time optimization that improves results while campaigns are running. Personalization at scale requires resources most teams don't have, forcing generic messaging that underperforms. Creative testing happens slowly through sequential experiments rather than parallel variations that surface winners faster.
