### The 8 Pillars of AI Transformation

To win with AI, you need more than tools. You need a sustainable, efficient, and profitable transformation plan.

### Build your dedicated AI team

Building a dedicated AI team requires a fully accountable leader supported by experts in product, strategy, research, and engineering working across departments under CEO leadership. Without this structure, companies chase tools opportunistically rather than strategically solving business problems, resulting in minimal financial impact.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)

### Deploy your AI Command Center

Selecting one AI command center platform (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude) company-wide prevents chaos from employees using personal accounts with company data. This investment delivers the highest ROI, yet most companies either haven't deployed licenses or underutilize features like projects or customGPTs.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)

### Create an AI-First Culture Shift

AI-first culture requires clear policies, CEO communication that addresses employee fears, and substantial change management as teams adapt their processes to AI systems. Success depends on deep user involvement and buy-in, and without cultural adoption, even the best AI strategy will fail regardless of technical execution.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)

### Connect & Enable your Tech Stack

Having an AI-enabled tech stack means ensuring your existing systems have API access so agents can push and pull data across fragmented platforms like CRMs and ERPs. It also involves auditing current tools for built-in AI features to avoid unnecessary purchases and maximize existing investments.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)

### AI-Healthy Data Assessment

AI requires clean, structured data because "garbage in, garbage out" applies to AI just as it does to humans, and messy data is difficult for both to search and reason about. Success means understanding where data is created, transformed, and stored across your organization to ensure AI systems can effectively process it.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)

### Build your Framework for AI Agent Readiness

AI agents function as digital employees that execute tasks across your systems, requiring clear SOPs, infrastructure access, and company templates to operate effectively. Like human workers, agents need ongoing monitoring, feedback, and training to improve performance, and they'll be significantly better six months in than on day one.

[Talk to an AI Strategist](/contact)[Learn more about AI Agents](/agents)

### Departmental AI Deep Dives

Departmental deep dives assess each team's people, processes, and technology to identify opportunities for AI, agentic workflows, or traditional automation. The goal is helping teams do more with less while shifting employees from tedious work to high-value strategic tasks they're energized by.

[Get started](/contact)[Departments We Improve](/departments)

### Find your 3-Year AI Vision

A three-year AI vision defines your company's dream state as the most scalable, AI-enabled organization in your category, providing clarity for stakeholders and a benchmark to measure progress. Like an annual budget, this roadmap ensures you're laddering up to bigger aspirations and can track when emerging technology aligns with your goals.
