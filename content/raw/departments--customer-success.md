## Why do these challenges exist?

Most customer success teams struggle with reactive churn prevention that addresses issues only after customers have already decided to leave. Health monitoring remains manual and inconsistent instead of automated scoring that surfaces risk before it compounds. Customer communication is time-intensive and can't scale beyond a small book of business without sacrificing personalization. Onboarding and adoption tracking rely on manual check-ins rather than automated workflows that ensure every customer reaches value quickly.

## Predictive Account Intelligence

Customer success teams are preventing churn by deploying AI that monitors usage patterns, predicts risk before customers disengage, and flags intervention opportunities while retention is still possible.

## Scaled Personalization
