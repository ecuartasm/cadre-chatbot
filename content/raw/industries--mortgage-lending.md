## Why do these challenges exist?

Most mortgage lenders struggle with manual underwriting and document review that slows loan approvals and creates borrower frustration during time-sensitive transactions. Income verification and asset documentation require extensive back-and-forth communication that extends timelines and risks deal fallout. Loan pricing and rate shopping comparisons happen manually, making it difficult to respond competitively when borrowers are evaluating multiple lenders simultaneously. Pipeline management relies on static reports rather than real-time alerts that surface loans at risk of falling out before intervention is possible.
