## Why do these challenges exist?

Most executives struggle with decision-making delays caused by information that's siloed across departments and systems. Business intelligence remains backward-looking through static reports rather than real-time dashboards that surface issues as they emerge. Strategic planning relies on manual data gathering and analysis instead of AI that synthesizes trends and scenarios instantly. Cross-functional alignment is time-intensive through endless meetings rather than automated briefings that keep leadership synchronized.
