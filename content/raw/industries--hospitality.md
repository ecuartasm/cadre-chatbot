## Why do these challenges exist?

Most hospitality operations struggle with reactive staffing that leads to guest service gaps during peak times or labor waste during slow periods. Revenue management and pricing remain manual and backward-looking instead of dynamic and responsive to real-time demand signals and competitive positioning. Guest communication is generic and time-intensive rather than personalized at scale with automated responses that maintain service quality. Operational coordination across housekeeping, maintenance, and front desk relies on manual handoffs that create service delays and guest dissatisfaction.
